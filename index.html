Effective date: 8 November 2026 · Assam, India

Northeast Samachar respects the privacy of its readers, followers, viewers and visitors. This policy explains how information may be handled when you interact with our Facebook Page and associated communication channels.

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy applies to interactions with the Northeast Samachar Facebook Page and communications we receive through channels associated with the page. It describes the information that may be made available to us, how we may use it, and how you can raise a privacy concern.

2. Information we may receive

Depending on how you interact with our page, we may receive information such as:

  • Your name and profile details that are publicly visible on Facebook.
  • Comments, reactions, shares, and other interactions with our posts.
  • Messages and enquiries sent to us through Facebook Messenger.
  • Information you choose to provide in feedback, news tips, photographs, videos, or other submissions.

Please do not send passwords, financial details, government identification numbers, or other sensitive personal information through public comments or ordinary messages.

3. How we use information

Information received through our page may be used to:

  • Respond to messages, questions, feedback, and complaints.
  • Review and, where appropriate, verify news tips or audience submissions.
  • Moderate comments and protect the page from spam, abuse, or misuse.
  • Understand audience engagement and improve our content.
  • Maintain the security and proper operation of our page.

4. Comments and user submissions

We welcome respectful discussion. We may hide or remove comments or submissions that appear to contain threats, harassment, hate speech, unlawful material, spam, scams, or personal information shared without permission.

Please ensure that you have the right to share any photograph, video, document, or other material you submit to us. Sending material does not guarantee that it will be published. If we intend to use a submission in a report, we may contact you for clarification or permission where appropriate.

5. Facebook and other third-party services

Our Facebook Page is hosted by Meta Platforms, Inc. Meta independently processes information about your use of Facebook under its own terms and privacy practices. We do not control Meta's systems, cookies, or data practices.

Please review Meta's Privacy Policy for details about how Facebook handles information.

6. Advertising and monetisation

Northeast Samachar may use or participate in advertising, content monetisation, or other eligible programmes offered by Facebook or Meta. Advertisements and related processing may be managed by Meta and are subject to Meta's applicable policies. We do not ask followers to provide payment-card or banking details to receive ordinary page updates or interact with our posts.

7. News reporting and public-interest material

As a digital news platform, Northeast Samachar may report on matters of public interest using sources such as official statements, press releases, public records, eyewitness accounts, and audience submissions. We aim to handle personal information responsibly and consider privacy, accuracy, relevance, and applicable law when preparing and publishing reports.

8. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell or rent personal information received through our page. We may share information when you ask us to, when it is reasonably necessary to respond to your enquiry or assess a submission, or when disclosure is required by applicable law or a lawful request from an appropriate authority.

9. Data security and retention

We take reasonable steps to limit access to information received through our communication channels. No online service or method of transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. We retain messages or submissions only for as long as reasonably needed for the purpose for which they were received, subject to legal or operational requirements.

10. Children's privacy

Our page is intended for a general audience and is not designed to collect personal information directly from children. Parents or guardians who have a concern about information concerning a child appearing on our page may contact us and request a review.

11. Your requests and choices

You may contact us to ask about information you have sent directly to the page or request that we remove a comment or submission where reasonably possible. We will consider requests in light of our legitimate reporting, record-keeping, safety, and legal obligations. Information held by Facebook itself must be managed through Facebook's own privacy and account tools.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The revised version will be published at this page with an updated effective date.
